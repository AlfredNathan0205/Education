## Limits the reviewer must know

1. **These are frameworks, not syllabi.** CISCE's Upper Primary documents give core concepts and learning outcomes for Classes VI-VIII together. Class VIII content is not a standalone section (Mathematics has a "CLASS -VIII" heading, p. 94). Any chapter or topic list for Grade 8 is therefore largely **OFFICIAL_INFERRED** and needs a written rationale, not OFFICIAL_EXPLICIT.
2. **Copies are mirrors.** A school mirror, an older results domain, and the Wayback Machine were used, because cisce.org was not reachable from the PM's location. The reviewer must compare each against the canonical cisce.org copy before verifying the pack.
3. **Currency for 2026-27 is not established.** The framework is First Edition, November 2016; modules are 2019 (Mathematics has no printed year). Whether any later edition supersedes them for 2026-27 must be confirmed with CISCE or the launch schools. An announced 2027-28 revamp was reported by the PM but not verified by the builder.
4. **Schools choose textbooks and sequencing.** Do not assume a universal sequence (spec rule).
